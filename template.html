How it works: Store enters POS burgers + physical closing bun count each evening. Buns sold = burgers × 2 · Expected closing = opening stock − buns sold · Variance = Expected − Actual. Positive = shortage (wastage, spoilage, staff meals, theft, unlogged use) · Negative = surplus (unlogged transfer-in, counting error, POS under-billing) · Tolerance: under 6 buns is treated as normal.
